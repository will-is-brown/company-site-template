Questions

Before you call.

The five things people ask us most often on the phone.

Do you charge for the diagnostic visit?
Yes — $189 for plumbing, $149 for heating and $169 for electrical. It comes off the final invoice if you go ahead with the repair, so it is effectively free when you hire us.
How fast can someone come out?
Routine work is usually scheduled within three to five business days. Emergency callouts are dispatched 24/7 and we aim to be on site within two hours anywhere inside Portland city limits.
Do you pull permits?
We do. Permits for panel work, circuit additions, repipes and most heating replacements are pulled and filed by us, and we schedule the inspection. You get a copy of the final sign-off for your records.
What does the guarantee cover?
Our workmanship is guaranteed for five years on the work we performed. Manufacturer warranties still apply on parts and equipment, and we handle those claims on your behalf.
Which areas do you cover?
Portland, Beaverton, Gresham, Lake Oswego and Tigard as our core area. If you are just outside it, call anyway — we often have a van scheduled nearby and the travel charge is fixed, not a surprise on the invoice.
